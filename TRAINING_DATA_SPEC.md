# Atlas Training Data Specification

## Sources

- RCA Reports
- Maintenance Records
- PLC Events
- SCADA Events
- Quality Reports

## Standard Format

Problem:
Observation:
Hypothesis:
Action:
Result:
Lesson:

## Example

Problem:
Motor vibration increased

Observation:
Current increased by 20%

Hypothesis:
Bearing degradation

Action:
Bearing inspection performed

Result:
Inner race wear confirmed

Lesson:
Rising vibration and current together indicate potential bearing wear.
